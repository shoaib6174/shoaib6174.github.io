# Review of plans A, B, C, and what the final plan takes from each

Reviewed against `docs/story/portrayal.md` (honest, diplomatic, agentic AI on robots leads) and `docs/story/evaluation-guide.md`.

## Plan A, the journal

**Strong.** The Journey entry template (date-noun heading, what happened, Chose / Ruled out / Result, proof, thread tags) is the best guard against self-narration in any of the three. Oldest-to-newest so decisions compound. Dimming toggles instead of hiding. "No proof, no listing."

**Weak.** Front-loads 2014 for a reader who wants current capability. Skill groups led with "ML and RL" rather than agentic AI. A separate Outside page is thin.

**Taken:** Journey template, ordering, year rail, dim-not-hide, proof rule, cut list for certificates and grades.

## Plan B, the workshop

**Strong.** "Why it exists" in two sentences on every object is the right unit of voice. The "Led to" chain lets the biography be read off the work. The Writing tick strip shows the learn-then-write rhythm without a sentence claiming it. Build fails on a skill row without proof; proof URLs link-checked in CI. The Shelf as "other people's work and what it led to".

**Weak.** Nine home sections is too long. Objects-only risks a repo list if the notes are flat. Cuts MLSA and IBM Z entirely; they belong as muted "studied/served" lines, not deletions. Leads with safe-rl-pe.

**Taken:** why-it-exists notes, Led-to chain, Writing tick strip, build-time proof check, link check, Shelf page, status labels on every object.

## Plan C, the three roles

**Strong.** "Studied" as a muted fourth column: certificates and failed attempts in the same format as achievements, without pretending to be achievements. One role per entry decided once. Hovering a role word dims the rest of the page. Shareable filter URLs. Cutting MSc CGPA and IELTS because showing one grade after hiding another invites the question.

**Weak.** Roles as the primary navigation turns the persona into a tagline, which plan C itself flags. Organises page is thin after 2022 and would look padded. A /years grid duplicates the Journey.

**Taken:** studied-column treatment, role-hover dimming on Home, one-role-per-entry rule, URL-backed filters, the grade cut.

## Decisions that override all three

1. **Agentic AI and vision-language models on robots lead** (Shoaib, 2026-10-09). Now, selected work, and the first skills row all open with the LLM robot task planner, VLM/VLA perception, and edge deployment. Pursuit-evasion RL is second. Business LLM systems are the same skill off-robot.
2. **Roles are weighted, not equal.** Builds is the headline. Explains is the second line. Brings people together is the third, stated once with current evidence, history on Journey.
3. **Six top-level pages, plus detail pages.** Home, Work, Journey, Writing, Skills, Shelf. University and Quantum are not pages: they are Journey entries, Work areas, and detail pages. Detail pages exist for any work item or journey entry that has more to show, and Shoaib will fill them with text and photos later.
4. **Setbacks in the same format as wins, one line each.** Never a paragraph.
5. **Numbers only where they stand alone.** No view totals. Per-article views on Writing for the top pieces only.
