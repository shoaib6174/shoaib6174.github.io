# MITx MicroMasters in Supply Chain Management, what it covered

Source: course descriptions pasted by Shoaib, 2026-10-10. He took it 2020 to 2021; the fifth course's lecture on machine learning in supply chains re-sparked the AI interest (inventory, facet 1). Whether the comprehensive exam and credential were completed: `[?]`.

| Course | Content |
|---|---|
| SC0x Supply Chain Analytics | Probability and statistics for variability, hypothesis testing, regression, mathematical optimisation for planning and network design, simulation, approximation methods. |
| SC1x Supply Chain Fundamentals | Demand forecasting, inventory replenishment policies, transportation routing and modal choice, segmentation of customers, products, and channels, uncertainty and risk. |
| SC2x Supply Chain Design | Network design: transportation, transshipment, facility location. **Supply chain finance: activity-based costing, working capital, cash-to-cash cycle, discounted cash flow.** Supplier management, procurement, contracts. Production planning, bills of materials, MRP. Sales and operations planning. |
| SC3x Supply Chain Dynamics | System dynamics modelling (the MIT method), complexity and interdependence, risk and resilience, international operations, efficiency versus flexibility trade-offs. |
| SC4x Supply Chain Technology and Systems | Data modelling, database design, SQL on supply chain data, **machine learning models for supply chain decisions**, ERP, WMS, TMS evaluation and implementation, emerging technology. |
