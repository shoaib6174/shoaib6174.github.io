I am an **AI & Robotics Engineer** passionate about building intelligent systems that bridge the gap between perception and action. My work spans **multi-modal models**, **computer vision**, and **scalable software architecture**.

Currently, I am a **Research Assistant** at the **NSU Intelligent Robotics Lab**, focusing on:
*   **AI for Robotics:** Integrating LLMs and VLLMs into robotic control pipelines.
*   **Simulation:** Developing high-fidelity environments in **NVIDIA Isaac Sim** and **Gazebo**.
*   **Reinforcement Learning:** Training agents for complex manipulation and navigation tasks.

### Professional Experience

My open-source journey includes:
*   **Google Summer of Code '22 (TensorFlow):** Implemented **Swin Video Transformer** models, converting weights from PyTorch to TensorFlow.
*   **DeepPavlov.ai Research Intern:** Developed dialogue graphs from the MultiWOZ dataset.
