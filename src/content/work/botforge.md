Built at FenloAI for a client. RAGChat ingests documents and answers with citations and a knowledge-gap flag when it cannot. VoiceBot Pro handles phone calls with real-time transcription, sentiment, and escalation. OmniBot puts WhatsApp, Telegram, a web widget, and Zapier into one inbox. Tracing and evaluation sit underneath so the client can see how the models are doing.
