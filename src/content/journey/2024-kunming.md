A large share of the newest robotics work I was reading came from Chinese universities, so I started reading about China itself: the longest continuously running civilisation, characters traceable back three thousand years, a long record of invention. The China-South Asia Bridge program took me to Yunnan University in Kunming for two weeks: classes on culture, traditions, the political system, and development, lab tours, museums. I passed HSK 1. Learning the language is how I am trying to understand the people behind the papers.
