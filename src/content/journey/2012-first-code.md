I grew up in a small town and had seen only a handful of computers before I got my own, in class 9. Within a few months I had decided that programmers were cool and I wanted to be one. I downloaded a book on C over a slow connection and worked through it, cover to cover. Then the board exams arrived and the computer went quiet for a while.
