During Google Summer of Code I read *Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!* and went looking for a course. A scholarship got me into Qubit by Qubit's summer school, run with researchers from MIT and Berkeley, and I spent the next year aiming at a PhD in quantum machine learning: the IBM Certified Associate Developer exam, the Womanium program, articles on QUBO and Grover's algorithm, a card game for teaching gates. I have been a Qiskit Advocate since 2023.
