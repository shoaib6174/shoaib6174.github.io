A large share of the newest robotics papers I was reading came from Chinese universities, and at some point I wanted to understand the people writing them, not just the papers. I started reading about China: the longest continuously running civilisation, characters you can trace back three thousand years, a long record of invention. Then the China-South Asia Bridge program took me to Yunnan University in Kunming for two weeks: classes on culture, traditions, the political system, and development, lab tours, museums. I passed HSK 1 and am working toward HSK 2. The language is the slow way in, and I think it is the only one.
