I had wanted mechanical engineering and robots. What I got was a textile degree with an engineering track, and I took the track for everything it offered: programming, electrical and mechanical engineering, automation and control, product design, SolidWorks and CATIA, operations research, simulation. The textile side, yarn, fabric, wet processing, apparel, never clicked, and I did not pretend it did. Of 166 credits, 73 were industrial engineering and management and 48 were textile. I graduated in 2022.
