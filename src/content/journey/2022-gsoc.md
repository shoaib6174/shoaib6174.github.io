I prepared with two TensorFlow specialisations and applied. Selected. Over the summer, with Luiz Gustavo Martins and Sayak Paul as mentors, I ported the Video Swin Transformer to TensorFlow 2, converted the weights from PyTorch, and published the models on TF Hub. They live on Kaggle Models now, with 2,636 downloads. Ten years after the C book, this was the first time something I had built was used by people I would never meet.
