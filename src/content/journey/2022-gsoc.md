I prepared with two TensorFlow specialisations and applied. Selected. Over the summer, mentored by Luiz Gustavo Martins and Sayak Paul, I ported the Video Swin Transformer to TensorFlow 2, converted the weights from PyTorch, and published the models on TF Hub. They are on Kaggle Models now, with 2,636 downloads. Eight years after the C book, it was the first time something I built was used by people I had never met.
