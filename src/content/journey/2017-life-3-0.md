In first year I read Max Tegmark's *Life 3.0*, and that is where the interest in AI started. The problem was that I could not yet code. I had already tried JavaScript, Java, C++, and competitive programming, each for a few weeks between semesters. After the book I tried CS50. Every attempt fizzled the same way: a start, a few exercises, then the term caught up with me. It took three years to learn that the fix was not another tutorial. It was making it the main thing.
