In first year I was class representative, debated, and joined the Business Club the year it was founded. My three-person team was runner-up in the intra-university debate championship, and I was best speaker in two rounds. Through the club I helped organise TEXBIZ, the university's first national competition, the first thing of that size I had run. I was vice president by 2021.
