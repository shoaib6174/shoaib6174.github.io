A friend's group had a facial-recognition attendance system as their Product Design project and could not get it working. In the last days before their deadline I built it with them on a Raspberry Pi. It was not my assignment. It was the first time code I wrote recognised something in the real world.

My own project in the same course was a radiation-protective phone cover. We worked through the process properly, from requirements to prototype, and the result was later published.
