A friend's group had a facial-recognition attendance system as their Product Design project, and a few days before the deadline it still did not work. I sat down with them and we built it on a Raspberry Pi. It was not my assignment, and it was the first time code I had written recognised something in the real world. Two years of false starts, and the thing that finally worked was someone else's homework.

My own project in the same course was a radiation-protective phone cover. We did the design process properly, requirements to prototype, and the result was later published.
