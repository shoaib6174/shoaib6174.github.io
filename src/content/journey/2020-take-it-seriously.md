Early 2020 I decided that this time learning to code would be the main thing. I finished a web development bootcamp run by an IPE graduate who had switched to software, and found I did not enjoy frontend work. Google's IT Support certificate filled some gaps in the basics. I took the cloud specialisation because DevOps was in demand, and finishing it told me DevOps was not for me either.

What mattered most was the MicroMasters in Supply Chain Management from MITx: five graduate-level courses over more than a year. Forecasting, inventory, transportation, network design, the finance of a supply chain, system dynamics, and the probability and statistics I had never had. One lecture in the fifth course was about machine learning in supply chains.
