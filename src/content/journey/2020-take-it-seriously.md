Early 2020. I decided that this time I would take it seriously. The bootcamp was run by an IPE graduate who had switched to software; I finished it and learned that I did not enjoy frontend work, and that I had gaps in computer science basics. Google's IT Support certificate filled some of the gaps. The cloud specialisation was there because DevOps was in demand; finishing it told me DevOps was not for me either.

The MicroMasters was the one that mattered. Five courses over more than a year, a mathematics foundation I had never had, and one lecture that pointed the whole thing at machine learning.
