Dr. Shahnewaz Siddique offered me a place in the lab to work on robotics software and AI. I had wanted to work on robots since before university, and this was the first place the work matched that. I spent several months on fundamentals first: control theory and optimal control, reinforcement learning, SLAM, diffusion and vision-language-action models, hardware. Then an LLM agent for a mobile robot with an arm, vision-language models on Jetson boards, a pursuit-evasion paper in preparation for CoRL 2026, and the AI payload for a CubeSat.
