The degree is called Textile Engineering. Less than a third of it was textile.

**Industrial engineering and management, 73 credits.** Engineering materials, machine shop, engineering drawing, AutoCAD and SolidWorks, manufacturing processes, fundamentals of mechanical engineering, automation and control, measurement and instrumentation, product design, CAD, CAM and CIM, CATIA, material handling and maintenance, operations management, operations research, ergonomics and safety, project and environment management, industrial simulation, supply chain management, marketing, accounting and cost management, quality management, industrial management, an industrial attachment, a project, and a comprehensive viva.

**Textile, 48 credits.** Polymer science, natural and man-made fibres, textile physics, testing and quality control, yarn manufacturing I and II, fabric manufacturing I and II, fabric structure and design, wet processing I and II, apparel manufacturing I and II.

**Fundamentals, 45 credits.** Mathematics I and II, physics, chemistry, computer programming, electrical and electronics engineering, statistics, business and communicative English, economics, sociology.
